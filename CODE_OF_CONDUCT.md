# ORION Community Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The ORION community welcomes people from all countries, backgrounds and experience levels.

We aim to create a respectful and inclusive environment.

## Expected Behaviour

- Be respectful and professional.
- Welcome beginners and experienced contributors.
- Provide constructive feedback.
- Respect different opinions.
- Give credit to original work.
- Communicate honestly.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Harassment or discrimination.
- Personal attacks or threats.
- Sharing private information without permission.
- Plagiarism or misrepresenting contributions.

## Reporting

Report inappropriate behaviour privately to the repository maintainer.

## Our Goal

Build a respectful international open-source community where people can learn, collaborate and improve ORION together.
